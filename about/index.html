About

I’m Vinay Chittora, a Rajasthan wildlife photographer and aspiring filmmaker. I work with a mobility disability, and that context has shaped a careful, low-impact field method built around patience and observation.

Why Cane & Camera

The cane is practical mobility support in the field, and a reminder to move slowly through habitat. The camera is a reporting tool — used to document behavior, landscapes, and conservation realities with clarity.

Rooted in Mukundara Hills

I grew up around Mukundara Hills Tiger Reserve, where scrub, forest edges, wetlands, and grasslands meet. That mosaic shaped how I read habitat, seasonality, and wildlife behavior, and it still informs every assignment.

How I work

My field practice is simple: patience, natural light, and minimal disturbance. I do not bait, crowd, or force interactions; I work for accurate, respectful documentation.

What I’m building

I’m building a long-term body of work across Wildlife, Documentaries, and conservation storytelling. The goal is a sustainable professional practice aligned with field ethics, scientific context, and public communication.

Work with me

I take on editorial assignments, NGO collaborations, licensing, screenings, and talks. If your project needs grounded field storytelling, get in touch here.
