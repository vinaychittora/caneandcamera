For wildlife filmmaking, cinematography support, field scouting, natural history documentation, and conservation storytelling collaborations in Rajasthan, including Mukundara-linked landscapes.

Project types

  • Wildlife documentary production and cinematography
  • Field scouting and story research support (MHTR / Mukundara / Rajasthan)

  • Location, timeline, and expected field days
  • Deliverables and usage scope
