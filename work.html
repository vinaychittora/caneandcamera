My work

Photographs, films, software and field data from Kota, Rajasthan. Most of it is about Mukundara Hills Tiger Reserve and the dry country around it.

Citizen science

Biodiversity of MHTR

I co-run an iNaturalist project that gathers observations from Mukundara Hills Tiger Reserve: plants, insects, reptiles, birds and mammals, each with a place, a date and an identification that others can check.

Open the project on iNaturalist

Open-source software

FALCON

Field Acoustic, Lens & Climate Observation Node. FALCON watches a place for animals with a camera, microphones and weather sensors, and analyses everything on your own hardware. Nothing goes to a cloud service.

It finds animals in the video, suggests the species, identifies bird calls, saves a clip of each visit and exports a clean dataset in the Camtrap DP standard for researchers.
